Copy trading simulation

A transparent, fully-documented, read-only simulation of copy-trading selected Hyperliquid traders. No real money is used.

What this experiment is

This board tracks three fictional portfolios of US$ 10,000.00 each. The session started on 23 Nov 2026 at 00:00 BRT. The engine follows public Hyperliquid traders in read-only mode and never sends orders, uses wallets, or uses keys.

Each portfolio has 25 sleeves (US$ 400.00 at the start). Fills are copied with an 800 ms delay, a 0.045% taker fee, and slippage that depends on the asset’s liquidity. Gross leverage is capped at 5× per sleeve. A sleeve is stopped if it falls 40% from its starting cash. The Screened book is re-evaluated every 7 days (next rescreen: 30 Nov 2026 at 00:00 BRT).

The rule: copy delay 800 ms, taker fee 0.045%, slippage by liquidity, 5× gross leverage cap, leader-scaled catastrophic stop-loss per sleeve (20–50%), de-risk to 50% at −15%, daily loss limit and coin/leverage caps (see Risk layer), rescreen every 7 days.

Restart note (25 Nov 2026). The portfolios were restarted on 23 Nov 2026 at 00:00 BRT, with US$ 10,000.00 each and no inherited leader positions, because the positions inherited at the original 20 Nov start had been booked at the leaders’ old entry prices instead of the market price, which inflated the units. Only positions the leaders opened or increased after 23 Nov 00:00 BRT are copied; closes of older positions are skipped, and a flip counts only from the new side. The history since 23 Nov was rebuilt from the leaders’ fills (Hyperliquid API plus the engine’s own logs; a few fills of six very active Top 25 leaders between 24 Nov 15:12 and 25 Nov 00:00 BRT are no longer held by any source, about US$ 20–140 of copied notional) and marked with market prices. The 20 Nov series is archived.

Daily performance

Every calendar day since the start is listed below, one row per day. The unit is a NAV per share that starts at 100.00 at each book’s start (all three books, Screened, Control and Top 25 (leaderboard): 23 Nov 2026, 00:00 BRT). Unit = equity / 10,000 × 100, with two decimal places and a decimal point. Period returns are compounded from the unit (ending unit / starting unit − 1). The day closes at midnight in São Paulo (00:00 America/Sao_Paulo, not UTC). Trades after midnight already belong to the new day. The current day is marked Partial and uses the latest snapshot. If capture pauses before midnight, the close stamp may be earlier than 23:59; the next snapshot after the gap is dated on its own BRT day.

Risk layer · hindsight vs walk-forward

Live in the simulation

Which risk settings would have worked best, and how much of that we could have known in advance. Hindsight picks the best rule using all data to date, so it always looks good. Walk-forward picks each day’s rule using only the days before it, then applies it to that day; that is the honest number, and the simulation runs the walk-forward pick, never the hindsight one. The gap between the two is the cost of overfitting. Score = return − max drawdown, averaged over Screened and Control. Recomputed every day after the 00:00 BRT close. How the rules work

Catastrophic stop-loss (leader-scaled): each sleeve is retired at 2× that leader’s own drawdown in the 30 days before we started copying, kept between 20% and 50% (40% if there is no history). It is measured on the full-size copy path (so a de-risk does not loosen it) and in the leader’s terms: when a leader runs above our 5× sleeve cap, the trigger is scaled by 5× ÷ leader leverage (floor 0.25). De-risk: at a −15% drawdown, sleeve size is cut to 50%. Re-risk: restored to full size if the leader’s path recovers 10% from its low. Daily loss limit: if a portfolio is down 3% since 00:00 BRT, exposure is cut to 50% until the next 00:00. Safety caps: net exposure per coin up to 50% of the portfolio, gross perp exposure up to 3× per portfolio and 5× per sleeve; leaders above 5× are copied scaled down, and a copy that would still break a cap is cut down or skipped and logged. Crowding: 5 or more leaders on the same side of one coin is logged as a warning. The walk-forward pick can change these values each day, inside fixed safety bounds.

Leverage (total-equity basis)

Leverage = gross perp notional ÷ total equity (perp + HIP-3 + spot). Many leaders keep collateral in spot, so their perp-account leverage looks higher than the leverage we actually copy. Portfolio gross leverage is our own book. Leaders above the 5× sleeve cap are copied at 5× ÷ their leverage on every fill (entries and exits), so the copy keeps the same mix of positions instead of skipping fills.

Top 25 (leaderboard)

This book copies the 25 names with the highest 30-day PnL on the official Hyperliquid leaderboard. There is no screening score. Vault, sub-account and agent addresses are excluded. It is rebalanced daily at 00:00 BRT, or on the first cycle after a pause. If a name leaves the top 25, its sleeve is closed at market, with costs, and the incoming name inherits that sleeve’s cash. The unit opened at 100.00 on 23 Nov 2026 at 00:00 BRT, together with the other two books.

Capture lag

How long it took to detect each fill in the recent window. Live WebSocket fills are typically faster than REST backfill. Hyperliquid’s official API allows at most 10 distinct users in WebSocket subscriptions per IP; the rest are followed over REST.

P&L by asset and by leader

Where each book’s profit and loss is coming from. The period filter defaults to All (the full session). Quick windows: 1D, 1W, 1M and 3M (trailing calendar days in BRT, from the session start). You can still pick a single day (00:00–23:59 BRT) or a from/to range. For a selected period, each row is the mark-to-market on that asset, leader or trade from the period’s opening NAV snapshot to its close (or now), net of fees, slippage and funding, so the rows add up to the book’s NAV change. All still shows the full session. Open the By trade tab for one row per trade. Click a column header (Item, P&L or N) to sort; the first click is descending.

Trades

A trade runs from the open (or from a position inherited at a leader change, at market price) until the position flats or flips. Times are in BRT. The period filter defaults to All. Use 1D, 1W, 1M or 3M for a trailing window in BRT, or pick a single calendar day or a from/to range. Closed trades are dated on their close day (00:00–23:59 BRT); open trades appear when the range includes today. Click a column header to sort.

Replacement history

When a leader is removed, the sleeve is closed at market, with costs, and the incoming name inherits that sleeve’s cash.
