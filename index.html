Copy trading simulation

A transparent, fully-documented, read-only simulation of copy-trading selected Hyperliquid traders. No real money is used.

What this experiment is

This board tracks three fictional portfolios of US$ 10,000.00 each. The session started on 20 Nov 2026 at 13:56 BRT. The engine follows public Hyperliquid traders in read-only mode and never sends orders, uses wallets, or uses keys.

Each portfolio has 25 sleeves (US$ 400.00 at the start). Fills are copied with an 800 ms delay, a 0.045% taker fee, and slippage that depends on the asset’s liquidity. Gross leverage is capped at 5× per sleeve. A sleeve is stopped if it falls 40% from its starting cash. The Screened book is re-evaluated every 7 days (next rescreen: 27 Nov 2026 at 13:56 BRT).

The rule: copy delay 800 ms, taker fee 0.045%, slippage by liquidity, 5× gross leverage cap, −40% stop per sleeve, rescreen every 7 days.

Daily performance

Every calendar day since the start is listed below, one row per day. The unit is a NAV per share that starts at 100.00 at each book’s start (Screened and Control: 20 Nov 2026, 13:56 BRT; Top 25 (leaderboard): 24 Nov 2026, 11:52:28 BRT). Unit = equity / 10,000 × 100, with two decimal places and a decimal point. Period returns are compounded from the unit (ending unit / starting unit − 1). The day closes at midnight in São Paulo (00:00 America/Sao_Paulo, not UTC). Trades after midnight already belong to the new day. The current day is marked Partial and uses the latest snapshot. If capture pauses before midnight, the close stamp may be earlier than 23:59; the next snapshot after the gap is dated on its own BRT day.

Exit rule

Leaders leave a book when they fail a rescreen, hit the −40% sleeve stop, or, in Top 25 (leaderboard), drop out of the official leaderboard. The 15% sleeve stop is no longer in force; the charts above keep it as a dashed reference.

Top 25 (leaderboard)

This book copies the 25 names with the highest 30-day PnL on the official Hyperliquid leaderboard. There is no screening score. Vault, sub-account and agent addresses are excluded. It is rebalanced daily at 00:00 BRT, or on the first cycle after a pause. If a name leaves the top 25, its sleeve is closed at market, with costs, and the incoming name inherits that sleeve’s cash. The unit opened at 100.00 on 24 Nov 2026 at 11:52:28 BRT.

Capture lag

How long it took to detect each fill in the recent window. Live WebSocket fills are typically faster than REST backfill. Hyperliquid’s official API allows at most 10 distinct users in WebSocket subscriptions per IP; the rest are followed over REST.

For a selected day, the table uses realized P&L of trades closed that day; open trades are included when the range covers the current day.
